# AI Adoption Readiness Brief

## Executive summary

This fictional report demonstrates headings, emphasis, lists, and tables without using customer or company data. A successful pilot should begin with a narrow workflow, measurable outcomes, and a clear human review step.

## Recommended pilot

1. Select one repeatable, low-risk internal workflow.
2. Define a baseline for time, quality, and rework.
3. Run a four-week pilot with named reviewers.
4. Compare results and document exceptions before expanding.

## Readiness checklist

- **Owner:** one accountable business lead
- **Data:** approved, non-sensitive pilot material
- **Review:** a human validates every external output
- **Exit criteria:** measurable improvement without higher error rates

| Dimension | Current state | Pilot target |
|---|---|---|
| Turnaround | 2 business days | Same day |
| Manual rework | 30% | Under 15% |
| Review coverage | Informal | 100% logged |

## Decision

Proceed with a limited pilot. Do not connect production systems or confidential datasets until the review and access-control requirements are approved.
