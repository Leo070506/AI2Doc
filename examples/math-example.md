# Mathematical Formula Example

This example verifies editable Word equations generated from common LaTeX syntax.

## Inline formula

Einstein's mass-energy relation is $E=mc^2$.

## Block formula

The quadratic formula is:

$$
x=\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}
$$

## Vector calculus

$$
\nabla \cdot E=\frac{\rho}{\epsilon_0}
$$

All formulas in the generated DOCX should remain editable as native Word equations.
